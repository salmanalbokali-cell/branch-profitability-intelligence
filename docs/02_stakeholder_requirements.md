# Stakeholder Requirements

## Stakeholder Scenario

This portfolio project uses a simulated business stakeholder environment to reflect how a real analytics engagement would begin after a business concern has been identified.

The primary stakeholder is **Daniel Morales, Chief Financial Officer (CFO)** of Maven Toys.

His initial request is:

> "Revenue and unit sales have grown compared with the same period last year, but gross-margin performance has weakened. I need to understand what is driving this gap and where management should prioritize corrective action."

## Primary Stakeholder

### Chief Financial Officer (CFO)

The CFO is the primary stakeholder because the main concern involves financial performance, gross profit, and margin deterioration.

The CFO needs to understand:

- Why gross profit is not growing at the same rate as revenue
- Where gross-margin deterioration is concentrated
- Which business factors are associated with the performance gap
- Which areas should receive management attention first
- Which performance indicators should be monitored going forward

## Supporting Stakeholders

### Operations Management

Needs to understand whether the performance gap is concentrated in particular stores, cities, or store-location types.

### Sales / Commercial Management

Needs visibility into product and category performance and whether changes in sales mix are affecting gross-margin results.

### Inventory Management

Needs to understand whether inventory availability and allocation are aligned with demand and financial contribution.

## Stakeholder Decision Needs

The analysis should support decisions related to:

- Prioritizing underperforming stores or business areas
- Reviewing product and category mix
- Identifying areas with weak gross-margin contribution
- Investigating inventory-related performance issues where relevant
- Establishing KPIs for continued performance monitoring

## Initial Analytical Questions

The stakeholder request is translated into the following analytical questions:

1. How did revenue, units sold, gross profit, and gross margin change between January–September 2022 and January–September 2023?
2. Which stores show the largest deterioration in gross-margin performance?
3. Which product categories and products contribute most to the change in gross profit and gross margin?
4. To what extent does sales mix differ between stronger- and weaker-margin stores?
5. Are inventory levels aligned with sales demand and financial contribution?
6. Which areas should management investigate first based on financial impact?

## SMART Question Refinement

The primary analytical question is:

> **Which stores, products, categories, and sales-mix changes contributed most to the decline in gross margin between January–September 2022 and January–September 2023, and where should management prioritize corrective action?**

### SMART Assessment

**Specific**  
Focuses on stores, products, categories, sales mix, and gross-margin performance.

**Measurable**  
Uses measurable indicators including revenue, units sold, gross profit, and gross margin.

**Action-oriented**  
The analysis is intended to identify where management should prioritize corrective action.

**Relevant**  
The question directly addresses the CFO's concern about weakening gross-margin performance.

**Time-bound**  
The comparison is between January–September 2022 and January–September 2023.

## Initial KPI Requirements

The project is expected to evaluate:

- Revenue
- Units Sold
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
- Gross Profit
- Gross Margin %
- Revenue Growth %
- Unit Growth %
- Gross Profit Growth %
- Gross Margin Change
- Average Selling Value
- Inventory Units
- Inventory Value

Final KPI definitions, formulas, assumptions, and data sources will be documented separately before formal analysis.

## Requirement Boundaries

The stakeholder requirements do not assume that any specific store, product, category, or inventory issue caused the gross-margin decline.

The purpose of the analysis is to test potential explanations using the available evidence.

Any limitations in the source data that prevent a stakeholder question from being answered reliably will be documented and communicated.
