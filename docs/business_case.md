# Business Case

## Portfolio Context

This is an independent data analytics portfolio project built using the publicly available Maven Toys dataset.

In a real organization, an analytics project would normally begin with a business signal, issue, or stakeholder request. Because confidential company data cannot be used or published for this portfolio, a public dataset was selected and a limited preliminary review was performed to identify a genuine business signal.

The stakeholder scenario used throughout this case study is therefore simulated, while the dataset, analytical work, findings, and methodology are real.

## Business Environment

Maven Toys is a fictitious multi-store toy retailer operating across Mexico. The available data covers stores, products, sales transactions, inventory, and calendar information.

Management needs reliable performance information to understand how effectively sales growth is translating into financial results across the business.

## Initial Business Signal

A limited preliminary review of the public dataset indicated that sales volume and revenue were growing, while gross-margin performance appeared to weaken.

This observation is treated only as a business signal at this stage, not as a conclusion about its cause.

## Simulated Stakeholder Request

For this case study, imagine that **Daniel Morales, Chief Financial Officer (CFO)**, reviews the company's performance and raises the following concern:

> "Sales and unit volume are growing, but gross-margin performance appears to be weakening. I need to understand what is driving this gap and where management should focus."

## Business Problem

The company appears to be achieving sales growth without the same level of improvement in gross-margin performance.

Management does not yet know which stores, products, categories, or other business factors may be contributing to this difference.

## Business Task

Investigate the factors associated with the weakening gross-margin performance, identify where the largest performance gaps exist, and provide evidence that can help management determine where corrective action should be prioritized.

## Analyst Role

The data analyst will transform the stakeholder concern into a structured analytical project, determine the required evidence, prepare and analyze the relevant data, communicate findings clearly, and support data-driven management decisions.

## Decision to Support

The analysis should ultimately help management decide:

- Where performance problems require the most attention
- Which business factors should be investigated or addressed
- Where management actions could have the greatest financial impact
